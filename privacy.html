Privacy notice

StudyDeck is a free study website with no adverts and no tracking. This page explains, in plain English, what it stores about you and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • To use StudyDeck you sign in with your email address (no password) and choose a username.
  • We store your email address, your username, your study progress and your "My decks" list. Nothing else.
  • You only appear on the leaderboard if you switch it on, and then other signed-in users see just your username and three numbers.
  • We never sell your data, show you adverts, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • You can delete your account at any time on the Stats page.

On your device

Your sign-in, your progress (which cards you know, your streaks, your best Match times), your "My decks" list and your light/dark setting are saved in your browser's own storage, so the site remembers you and works offline. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them from that device (your account keeps its copy). This storage is only used to make the site work, so there is no cookie banner.

On our servers

We store:

  • Your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code and recognise you.
  • Your study progress and your "My decks" list, so they are the same on all your devices.
  • A username you choose, and whether you want to be on the leaderboard.
  • Technical sign-in records (such as the time you signed in and your IP address), kept for a short time by our database provider to keep accounts secure.

We use this only to run your account and the site's features (signing in, keeping your progress the same on your devices, and the leaderboard if you switch it on). You can stop at any time by deleting your account.

The leaderboard

The leaderboard is off unless you switch it on (Stats page, "Show me on the leaderboard"). When it's on, other signed-in users can see your username, your current streak, how many cards you've mastered and how many answers you gave in the last 7 days. They never see your email address. Please don't use your real name as your username. You can switch it off or change your username at any time.

Who stores it

  • Supabase stores accounts and progress, on servers in Ireland (EU).
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails, from servers in Ireland (EU).
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare deliver the website itself. Like any website host, they see your IP address when you visit.

Some of these companies are based in the United States and may process data there, under their own data protection safeguards.

How long we keep it

Your email address and progress are kept until you delete your account. Accounts that haven't been used for two years may be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you.

  • Delete everything yourself: sign in, go to Stats and press Delete my account. This removes your email address, username and online progress straight away.
  • See a copy of your progress: press Export progress on the Stats page.
  • Anything else: email hello@studydeck.uk.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

StudyDeck is made for school students. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or carer before signing up.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
